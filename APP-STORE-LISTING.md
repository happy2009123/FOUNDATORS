## App Name

## Short Description (80 chars)
The social network for founders, developers, and builders.

## Full Description

**Turn your ideas into real products.**

Foundators is the social platform where entrepreneurs, programmers, designers, and visionaries connect, collaborate, and build the future together.

**WHY FOUNDATORS?**

Unlike generic social networks, Foundators is built specifically for builders. Every feature is designed to help you find the right people, skills, and opportunities to turn your next idea into something real.

**KEY FEATURES**

🏠 **Smart Feed** — Discover ideas, updates, and opportunities from founders and builders worldwide.

🎬 **Reels** — Watch quick-fire content from the builder community. Get inspired, learn, and share your journey.

💬 **Direct Messages** — Real-time conversations with builders, founders, and potential collaborators. Find people by their unique Foundators ID.

🔍 **People Search** — Find co-founders, developers, designers, and mentors by name, handle, or role.

🎯 **Foundators Match** — Tell us what you need — a co-founder, programmer, funding, mentor — and we'll connect you with the right people.

📊 **Startup Profiles** — Follow innovative startups, track their growth, and explore collaboration opportunities.

🎨 **Gestures** — Create and share animated digital gestures — birthday cards, thank you notes, congratulations, and more. Collaborate with others in real-time.

📐 **Gesture Templates** — Browse 30+ professionally designed animated templates. Fork, remix, and customize them.

🤝 **Collaborative Gestures** — Work together with others on gesture projects. Sign, review, and create as a team.

🏆 **Gesture Community** — Share your creations, discover trending gestures, and follow your favorite creators.

💡 **Ideas Board** — Share your startup ideas, get feedback, and find co-founders who believe in your vision.

🏆 **Challenges** — Compete in weekly builder challenges. Ship products, solve problems, and earn recognition.

📅 **Events** — Discover hackathons, meetups, and networking events in your area and online.

🤖 **AI Assistant** — Get help with your ideas, business plans, and technical questions.

🔐 **Privacy First** — Your data stays on your device. End-to-end encryption coming soon.

**BUILT FOR BUILDERS**

✅ Proof-of-work profiles — show what you've built, not just what you say
✅ Unique Foundators IDs — find and connect with anyone instantly
✅ Haptic feedback — tactile experience on every interaction
✅ Dark mode by default — easy on the eyes during late-night building sessions
✅ Offline support — access your content even without internet
✅ PWA ready — install on any device, works like a native app

**WHO IS IT FOR?**

• Founders looking for co-founders and early team members
• Programmers seeking exciting projects and startups
• Designers wanting to collaborate on innovative products
• Investors scouting the next big thing
• Anyone with an idea who wants to build something real
